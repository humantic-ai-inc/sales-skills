# Buyer Read

Tells you how one person communicates, what they respond to and what they will worry about, before you reach out or walk into a call.

**Say something like:** "Who is this and how should I approach them? [LinkedIn URL]" or "I have a call with [LinkedIn URL] in two minutes."

**It also fixes a bad profile.** If the read comes back thin, it asks you for a bio or call notes and improves it. If the person changed jobs, it rebuilds the profile. If it is the wrong person, it records the correction.

**Works without Humantic** on what you know about the person, and gets much sharper with Humantic connected.
