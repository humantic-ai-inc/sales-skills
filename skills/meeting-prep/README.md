# Meeting Prep

A one-page brief for a specific sales meeting: the outcome it has to produce, how to talk to each person in the room, what changed at the account, and the ask plus a fallback.

**Say something like:** "Prep me for my meeting with [company] tomorrow. Attendees are [names and LinkedIn URLs]."

**Handles** discovery calls, renewal conversations, group meetings where people want different things, and the two-minute version when you are already late.

**Works without Humantic** from your calendar and CRM, and gets a read on every attendee with Humantic connected.
