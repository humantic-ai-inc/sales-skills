# Buying Committee

Maps the people who decide on your deal and works out how to move them as a group: who to approach first, where they will split, how to equip your champion, and who is missing.

**Say something like:** "Map the buying committee on the [company] deal: [names, roles, LinkedIn URLs]." or "My champion is being overruled by their VP. What do I do?"

**Works without Humantic** from your CRM and notes, and adds a read on every member with Humantic connected.
