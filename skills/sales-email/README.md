# Sales Email

Writes the email for the person reading it: first pitches, rewrites, replies, follow-ups, one message to several stakeholders, and post-meeting notes.

**Say something like:** "Write a first email to [LinkedIn URL]. I'm leading with [pain point]. I'm [who you are]." or "They went quiet. Here's my last email: [paste]."

**It will also tell you when an email should not be sent yet**, and suggest a better reason to reach out.

**Works without Humantic** on proven email craft, and matches each email to its reader with Humantic connected.
