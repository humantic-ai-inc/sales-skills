# Expansion Play

Finds what else an existing customer should be buying, who to raise it with and when. It checks the current deployment is working first, because asking for more on a struggling rollout puts the renewal at risk.

**Say something like:** "Where can we expand at [company]? They use us in [team] today."

**Works without Humantic** from your CRM and usage data, and times the ask to real buyer signals with Humantic connected.
